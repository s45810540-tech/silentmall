# SILENT MALL: THE LAST NIGHT

## Premise
At 11:47 PM, the mall seals itself with the player still inside. The building is not simply haunted: it has preserved the final minutes of a long-hidden evacuation and now repeats them. Every sound gives the Listener a trail. The player must recover a keycard, open the shutter, and cross the mall while uncovering why the doors were sealed.

## Chapter 1 — The Closing Shift
**Entity: The Listener**
The Listener was the night security guard. During the evacuation, he heard his partner calling from the dark corridors and stayed behind to find her. The mall learned the sound of her footsteps and began using it to lure him. He now follows every noise, convinced that the next sound will lead him home.

Gameplay role: primary sound-hunting threat. Loud movement, impacts and thrown objects draw him toward the player. The main atrium is the introductory route.

Cutscene: an animated shutter-closing sequence establishes the sealed mall; the public-address system insists the building is not empty.

## Chapter 2 — Below the Food Court
**Entity: The Usher**
Elias Vale maintained the service corridors. When workers discovered the sealed cinema exit, management ordered him to redirect everyone away from it. He obeyed until the alarms stopped and the doors locked. He now appears wherever a sign points toward safety.

Gameplay role: false directions and misleading announcements. The Usher periodically sends the hunter toward a false announcement point while the player crosses a distinct service-tunnel floor plan. The player finds the next keycard in the food-court wing.

Cutscene: an animated service-lift arrival and moving emergency light introduce the basement; an old maintenance log reveals Elias's final shift was never closed.

## Chapter 3 — The Children's Wing
**Entity: The Static Child**
Anya was separated from her family during the evacuation. Security cameras recorded her in places she could not have reached. She learned to hide inside the interference between frames. Her paper wristband is dated tomorrow.

Gameplay role: apparition and psychological warning. The Static Child phases out of view, distorts the image, and reappears near the player's previous position. Her appearances signal that the mall is manipulating the player's sense of direction. The chapter uses a separate toy-wing aisle layout.

Cutscene: an animated security-camera interference sequence reveals Anya's wristband and a voice asking why nobody came back.

## Chapter 4 — The Heart of the Mall
**Entity: The Night Manager**
Mr. Vale signed the final closure order and concealed the blocked cinema exit. He believes the mall must remain full, lit and open forever. He does not chase; he schedules. Every person inside is another shift that must be completed.

Gameplay role: final escalation on a distinct management-floor layout. Opening the final shutter starts the Night Manager's boss phase: the hunter locks onto the player and the pursuit accelerates while the mall's announcements become personal.

Cutscene: an animated executive-floor reveal shows the sealed exit and the manager's role in the repeating evacuation night.

## The fifth entity — The Mall
The building absorbed the evacuation as a repeating memory. Corridors, announcements and locked doors are its nervous system. It keeps the missing as echoes and recruits the living to complete the shift.

Gameplay role: overarching antagonist expressed through darkness, sound, environmental changes and recurring spaces.

## Implemented game scope
- Four sequential chapters with four distinct floor-plan layouts: main atrium, service tunnels, toy-wing aisles and management floor.
- Chapter-specific facade dressing, lighting positions, keycard locations and environment tinting.
- The Listener's sound-tracking hunt, the Usher's false-announcement diversion, the Static Child's phase-and-reappear behaviour, and the Night Manager's final accelerated boss phase.
- Animated CSS cinematic sequences with moving silhouettes, camera drift, lighting sweeps, letterbox bars and chapter-specific captions, in addition to the readable story panels.
- The fifth entity, The Mall, remains the overarching antagonist expressed through environment, sound, lighting and the final lockdown.

## Production notes
The cinematics use original in-game CSS/Phaser presentation rather than pre-rendered video. Character voice acting and bespoke animated character sprite sheets are not included. The current entity appearances reuse the existing pixel-art hunter sprite; the chapter-specific behaviours and map layouts are implemented in code.
